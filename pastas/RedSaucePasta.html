Red Sauce Pasta

Serving: 2

Time: 30 mins.

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup pasta
  • 2 medium tomatoes, pureed
  • 2 garlic cloves, chopped
  • 1 small onion, chopped
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 tablespoon tomato ketchup (optional)
  • Salt, chilli flakes, and oregano to taste
  1. Boil the pasta in salted water until soft, then drain.
  2. Heat olive oil in a pan and sauté the garlic and onion.
  3. Add tomato puree, salt, chilli flakes, and oregano.
  4. Cook the sauce for 5–7 minutes. Add ketchup if desired.
  5. Add the boiled pasta and mix well.
  6. Serve hot with grated cheese if you like.
