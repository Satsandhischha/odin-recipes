Lasagna

Serving: 4

Time: 30-40 mins.

Ingredients:

  • 6 lasagna sheets
  • 1 cup tomato pasta sauce
  • 1 cup grated mozzarella cheese
  • 1/2 cup white sauce
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • Salt, oregano, and chilli flakes to taste
  • 1/2 cup sautéed vegetables (optional)
  1. Cook the lasagna sheets according to the packet instructions.
  2. Preheat the oven to 180°C.
  3. Spread a thin layer of tomato sauce in a baking dish.
  4. Add a layer of lasagna sheets, tomato sauce, white sauce, and cheese.
  5. Repeat the layers until the ingredients are used up.
  6. Finish with white sauce and a generous layer of cheese.
  7. Bake for 25–30 minutes until the cheese melts and turns golden.
  8. Let it rest for 5–10 minutes before cutting and serving.

Tip: If you don't have an oven, you can use a covered, heavy-bottomed pan on very low heat, provided your baking dish is suitable for stovetop use.
