White Sauce Pasta

Serving: 2

Time: 30 mins.

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup pasta
  • 1 tablespoon butter
  • 1 tablespoon all-purpose flour (maida)
  • 1 cup milk
  • 2 tablespoons grated cheese (optional)
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • 1/2 teaspoon oregano
  • Chopped vegetables(optional)
  1. Boil the pasta until soft, then drain.
  2. Melt butter in a pan over low heat.
  3. Add flour and stir for about one minute.
  4. Slowly pour in the milk while stirring to prevent lumps.
  5. Add salt, black pepper, oregano, and cheese.
  6. Cook until the sauce becomes creamy.
  7. Add the pasta and mix well. Serve hot.
